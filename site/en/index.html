Laboratorio digital

Interactive exhibits for exploring one idea directly. Each is a single file with no dependencies, and its own text, drawings and code.

Español

  • Orders of magnitude

    From the Planck length to the observable universe in a single zoom, with more than a hundred objects drawn to scale with one another.

  • La misma Luna

    The whole planet sees the same phase of the Moon; what changes from place to place is how it appears rotated. Move an observer around the Earth and see for yourself. In Spanish for now.

  • El espectro

    All of light, from radio waves to gamma rays, falling on the Earth. Travel along the dial and see which part makes it to the ground. In Spanish for now.
